# Glossary: episode 2

- **Developer Edition org**: A free Salesforce org for learning and testing. It is not a company's live system.
- **Salesforce DX project**: A folder of source files (objects, fields, flows) that can be sent to an org and kept in git. `sfdx-project.json` marks the folder.
- **Revenue Settings**: The Setup page that turns the Revenue Cloud features on or off.
- **Pricing procedure**: The ordered steps that work out a price on a quote line. Revenue Cloud starts with a default one.
- **Sync pricing data**: A button that refreshes the copies of price data that the pricing steps read. Run it after every price change.
