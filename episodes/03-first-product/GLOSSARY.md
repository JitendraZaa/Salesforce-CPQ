# Glossary: episode 3

- **Product**: Anything the company sells. Here the Volt Van, an electric panel van.
- **Product selling model**: How a product is sold over time: one time, for a fixed term, or until cancelled. The model is chosen per price, not per product.
- **One-time**: Sold once and paid once. The Volt Van uses it. Term-defined (a subscription with an end date) and evergreen (a subscription with no end date until the customer cancels) are used from episode 7.
- **Price book**: A list of products with prices. The standard price book holds the base price of every product.
- **Price book entry**: One product's price, with its selling model, in one price book.
- **List price**: The published price before any discount or adjustment.
- **Catalog**: The organised list of what the company sells, which a rep browses on a quote.
- **Category**: A folder inside a catalog, such as Vehicles.
