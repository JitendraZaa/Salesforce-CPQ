# Glossary: episode 4

- **Lead**: A person or company that has shown interest and might buy.
- **Lead conversion**: Turning a lead into an account, a contact and, if chosen, an opportunity.
- **Opportunity**: One specific chance to sell, with a stage, an amount and a close date.
- **Quote**: A priced offer to a customer. Here it hangs from the opportunity.
- **Quote line**: One product, quantity and price on a quote.
- **Browse Catalogs**: The button on a quote that opens the catalog so the rep can pick products.
- **Product index**: The searchable copy of the catalog that Browse Catalogs reads. It must be rebuilt after products or categories change.
- **Line editor**: The Lines tab of a quote, where lines are shown and edited.
