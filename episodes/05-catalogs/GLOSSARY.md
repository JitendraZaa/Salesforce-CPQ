# Glossary: episode 5

- **Product Catalog Management (PCM)**: The part of Revenue Cloud that holds products, attributes, bundles, catalogs and categories.
- **Category and subcategory**: A folder in a catalog and a folder inside a folder. Vans is a subcategory of Vehicles.
- **Assigning a product to more than one category**: One product can appear in several categories, such as the Wall Charger in Home and Cars.
- **SKU and product code**: A short code that identifies one sellable item, such as VV-100.
- **Product family**: A broad group of products used in lists and reports, such as Vehicles.
- **Guided selling**: A way of helping a rep choose products by asking questions. Named here, not built.
- **Partial index rebuild**: Updating only what changed in the product index, which is faster than a full rebuild.
