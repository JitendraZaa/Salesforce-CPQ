# Glossary: episode 6

- **Attribute**: A characteristic of a product that is chosen during the sale, such as Battery or Body.
- **Attribute picklist**: The list of allowed values for an attribute, such as Standard 60 kWh and Long Range 90 kWh. One value is the default.
- **Product classification**: A template that gives similar products the same attributes. Electric Vehicle holds Battery and Body.
- **Attribute inheritance**: A product based on a classification receives that classification's attributes without being set up one by one.
