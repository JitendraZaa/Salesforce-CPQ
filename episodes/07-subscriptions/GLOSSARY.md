# Glossary: episode 7

- **Subscription**: A product charged again for each period while it runs.
- **Recurring charge**: The amount charged each period of a subscription.
- **Pricing term versus subscription term**: The period one price covers (1 month) versus how long the service runs (12 months, from the line's start and end dates).
- **Proration policy**: The rule for charging only part of a period when a customer starts or stops partway through it.
- **Product selling model option (PSMO)**: The record that says a product may be sold with a certain selling model; one option is the default.
- **MRR (monthly recurring revenue)**: Recurring contract value in one month. Velora's rule counts subscriptions only.
- **ARR (annual recurring revenue)**: MRR times 12, a run rate.
- **TCV (total contract value)**: All committed charges over the contract term, one-time and recurring.
- **SOQL**: Salesforce Object Query Language, a way to read records with a query. Evergreen (no end date) is recalled here from episode 3.
