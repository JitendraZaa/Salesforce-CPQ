# Glossary: episode 8

- **Bundle**: Products sold together as one unit. A configurable bundle lets the rep change what is inside.
- **Configurable bundle**: A bundle whose components the rep can choose in the configurator, within the limits set on the groups.
- **Product group**: A box inside a bundle that holds components and states how many may be chosen (minimum and maximum).
- **Required and optional component**: A required component is always in the bundle; an optional one is added only if chosen.
- **Group cardinality and local cardinality**: The minimum and maximum number of components chosen in a group, and the minimum and maximum quantity of one component.
- **Quantity scaling (proportional, constant)**: Proportional means the component quantity follows the bundle quantity. Constant means it stays the same.
- **Included versus separately priced**: Whether the bundle's own price already covers a component, or the component carries its own price.
- **Add-on and cross-sell**: An add-on is an extra bought with a product. A cross-sell is a related product offered to the buyer.
