# Glossary: episode 10

- **Qualification rule**: A rule that decides which customers may see or buy a product.
- **Disqualification rule**: A rule written the other way round: the conditions under which a product is hidden.
- **Decision table**: A lookup table that rules and prices read. It must be refreshed after it changes.
- **Context definition**: The map of data (account, quote, lines) that rules and pricing read.
- **Qualification procedure**: The ordered steps that run the qualification rules when a rep browses the catalog.
