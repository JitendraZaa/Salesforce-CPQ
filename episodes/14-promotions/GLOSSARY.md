# Glossary: episode 14, Promotions

Terms introduced in this episode. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Promotion**: A rule-based discount for eligible products, applied automatically or with a coupon code.
- **Coupon**: A code a customer or rep enters to switch a promotion on.
- **Promotion Execution element**: The pricing procedure step that applies promotion discounts during price calculation.
- **Rule library**: The list that maps context tags to the fields used to define promotions and eligibility.
- **Context tag**: A named label for a field in the context definition, used by rules and promotions.
- **Eligibility**: The conditions a customer, product or quote must meet for a promotion to apply.
- **Worked example**: A calculation shown on a slide to teach a rule, labelled as not coming from Salesforce.
