# Glossary: episode 16, Approvals

Terms introduced in this episode. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Manual discount**: A discount the rep types in during the deal.
- **Line versus header discount**: A line discount applies to one line; a header discount applies to the whole quote.
- **Cost, cost book, unit cost**: Cost is what a product costs Velora. A cost book lists costs by period. Unit cost is the cost of one unit on a line.
- **Margin and markup**: Margin = (price - cost) / price. Markup = (price - cost) / cost.
- **Floor price, margin floor**: The lowest price, or lowest margin, allowed without an exception.
- **Approval workflow**: A required yes before a quote goes further.
- **Advanced Approvals**: The Salesforce approval feature, built on flows, that handles multi-step approvals.
- **Flow Approval Process**: A flow that models an approval as stages and steps.
- **Stage and approval step**: A stage is a group of steps; an approval step asks a person or group to decide.
- **Approval work item**: The task given to an approver.
- **Approver**: The person or group who decides.
- **Approval chain (serial versus parallel)**: Approvers asked one after another, or all at once.
- **Smart approvals and delegation**: Smart approvals skip steps that need no new review on resubmission. Delegation lets an approver hand approvals to someone else for a time.
- **Persona**: A typical job, used to recommend which permission sets to assign.
- **Permission set licence**: A licence that must be held before certain permission sets can be assigned.
