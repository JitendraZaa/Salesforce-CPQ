# Glossary: episode 18, Quote versions and the primary quote

Terms introduced in this episode. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Quote version**: One of several copies of a quote for the same deal, each with its own lines and price.
- **Revision**: A changed version of a quote made after feedback.
- **Clone (Deep Clone)**: Copying a quote with its lines into a new Draft quote. Approval work items are not copied.
- **Primary (synced) quote**: The one quote whose lines are copied to the opportunity's products.
- **Quote status**: The stage of the quote, such as Draft, Needs Review, Approved or Presented.
- **Expiration date**: The last day the offer can be accepted.
- **Acceptance**: The customer's agreement to the quote.
- **E-signature**: A signature given electronically. Document Builder's signature component is limited to orgs with Field Service.
- **Smart approvals**: On resubmission, steps that need no new review are approved automatically.
