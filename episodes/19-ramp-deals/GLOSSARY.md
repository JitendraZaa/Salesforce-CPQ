# Glossary: episode 19, Ramp deals

Terms introduced in this episode. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Ramp deal**: A multi-period deal where price, quantity or discount change at set points.
- **Segment**: One period of a ramp deal, here one year.
- **Ramp schedule**: The set of segments of one ramp deal.
- **Group ramp**: A ramp applied to a group of lines, used here because a line ramp is not allowed inside a bundle.
- **Uplift**: A planned percentage price increase from one segment to the next.
- **Compound uplift**: An uplift applied to the already uplifted price, so 3 percent twice is 1.03 x 1.03.
- **MRR and ARR**: Monthly and annual recurring revenue; Velora counts recurring charges only.
- **TCV and ACV**: Total contract value (all charges over the term) and annual contract value (TCV divided by contract years).
- **CMRR**: Contracted monthly recurring revenue for a given month.
- **Sales Transaction Line Editor**: The single-grid line editor that supports ramp deals for groups.
