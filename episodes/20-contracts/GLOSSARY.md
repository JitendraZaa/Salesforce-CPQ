# Glossary: episode 20, Contracts and clauses

Terms this episode introduces. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Contract**: The agreement that governs what was bought, for how long, on what terms.
- **CLM (contract lifecycle management)**: Creating, negotiating, signing and maintaining agreements.
- **MSA (master service agreement)**: The umbrella legal terms between two companies, under which single purchases are made.
- **Order form**: The short document for one purchase, usually under an MSA.
- **SOW (statement of work)**: The description of the services to deliver.
- **Redlining**: Marking changes to contract wording during negotiation.
- **Clause**: A piece of reusable contract text, such as a warranty.
- **Clause library**: The place where the approved clauses are kept and found.
- **Placeholder token**: A blank inside a clause, such as the number of years, filled in for each deal.
- **Quote special term**: A clause added to one quote from the clause library. The same clause cannot be added twice.
- **Contract activation**: Setting the contract's status to Activated. An active contract can no longer have its prices edited or deleted.
- **Application usage assignment (AUA)**: The tag that marks a contract as belonging to Revenue Management. Here it is RevenueLifecycleManagement, and later amendments, renewals and asset links depend on it.
