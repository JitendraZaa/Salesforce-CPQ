# Glossary: episode 21, Negotiated prices that last

Terms this episode introduces. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Customer price**: The price a particular customer pays, which can differ from the list price.
- **Contract price**: A price fixed on a contract for a product, for a stated period.
- **Price agreement (contract-based pricing)**: An agreed price on a contract that quotes and orders for that customer then use automatically.
- **Contract item price**: The record on a contract that holds the price for one product, selling model and date range.
- **Cumulative quantity pricing**: Choosing the volume tier from the total bought over time, counting quantities already in service as well as the new order. It needs contract volume tiers and is shown on a slide only.
