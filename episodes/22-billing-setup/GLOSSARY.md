# Glossary: episode 22, Billing setup

Terms this episode introduces. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Billing**: The part of Revenue Management that turns activated orders into invoices.
- **Billing policy**: The rules on a product that say how its order lines are invoiced.
- **Billing treatment**: The setting inside a policy that chooses billing details for a legal entity; one is the default.
- **Billing treatment item**: One line of a treatment, with its billing type (advance or arrears) and processing order.
- **Frequency**: How often a line is invoiced, for example monthly.
- **Billing in advance versus in arrears**: Invoicing before the period starts, or after it ends.
- **Period boundary**: The rule that fixes where a billing period starts and ends, such as the anniversary of the start date or the calendar month.
- **Billing profile**: A customer's billing preferences: bill day, billing contact, address. Each one is a Billing Account record.
- **Bill day of month (BDOM)**: The day of the month on which a customer is invoiced.
- **Payment term**: The agreed number of days to pay after the invoice date.
- **Net 30**: A payment term of 30 days after the invoice date.
- **Sold-to, bill-to, ship-to**: The buying party, the party and address that are invoiced, and the delivery address.
- **Legal entity**: The company in a group that issues the invoice. Milestone billing and billing arrangement (invoicing at project stages, and splitting invoices across billing accounts) are named on a slide only.
