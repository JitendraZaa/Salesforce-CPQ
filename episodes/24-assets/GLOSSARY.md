# Glossary: episode 24, Assets and activation

Terms this episode introduces. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Activation**: Setting an order to Activated. After that it cannot be edited, and the assets and billing schedules are made by the flows.
- **Assetizable**: A product setting that allows an activated order to turn it into an asset.
- **Asset**: The record of a product or service the customer has purchased.
- **Installed base**: All the assets a customer has.
- **Subscription versus asset**: The subscription is what was sold over time; the asset is the record of what the customer has after the order.
- **Assetization**: Creating assets from an order.
- **Asset action**: An event that changes an asset, such as the initial sale.
- **Asset state period and its MRR**: A span of time in which an asset's quantity and price are constant, and the monthly recurring revenue of that span.
- **Managed Asset Viewer (MAV)**: The Assets tab on the account where assets are listed and changes start.
- **Billing schedule**: The record, one per activated order line, that holds what is to be invoiced over the line's life.
- **Billing schedule group**: All billing schedules from one asset, including amendments.
