# Glossary: episode 27, Usage setup

Terms this episode introduces. The full project glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the repository root.

- **Usage-based product**: A product charged by how much is used.
- **Anchor product and pack product**: The main usage subscription; an add-on that grants more units.
- **Usage resource**: The thing that is measured, here Charging Energy.
- **Unit of measure (UoM) and UoM class**: The unit (kWh) and the group of related units it belongs to.
- **Product usage resource**: The link between a product and a usage resource, with its policies.
- **Usage aggregation policy**: The rule that combines a period's readings into one quantity, for example a monthly sum.
- **Rate**: The price per unit of usage.
- **Rate card**: The list that holds rates.
- **Rate card entry**: One rate on a rate card for a product, usage resource and date range.
